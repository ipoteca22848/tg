# Gift artwork

These are preview images of Telegram gifts, sourced from the public mirror:
https://github.com/denzyve/cdn-telegram-gifts

Per-file source URLs are recorded in catalog.json. Original Telegram gift artwork belongs to its respective rights holders. Companion is unofficial and not affiliated with Telegram.

These pictures are used as visual previews for a fictional internal gift economy. A purchase in Companion does not buy a Telegram gift, NFT, Telegram Stars or real TON, and does not confer ownership of the artwork or an on-chain asset. Prices are fictional application values, not market prices.
